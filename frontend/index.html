Upload Geotagged Plant Photo

The location is read automatically from the photo's GPS data or from a printed location stamp (e.g. GPS Map Camera), then checked against the Van Udyan boundary. Afterwards you can ask the AI to suggest the species.

📸 Tips for accurate AI identification
  • Go close: one leaf, flower or fruit should fill most of the photo. Photos of a whole sapling from a few metres away rarely get identified.
  • Keep the background plain (soil, sky or your hand), not grass or other plants.
  • Hold the phone steady in daylight so the leaf veins are sharp. Avoid harsh shadows.
  • Flowers and fruits identify best. If there are none, photograph the top side of a mature leaf and select Leaf above.
  • The GPS Map Camera box is removed automatically before identification.
